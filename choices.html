Choose Your Impact

Every Gift Creates Lasting Change

Every Gift Creates Lasting Change At ToCAWI (Tails of Care Animal Welfare Initiative), every contribution helps protect animals, safeguard public health, and strengthen communities across Kenya. Whether you're funding a life-saving rabies vaccination, sponsoring a sterilization, supporting community education, or equipping our volunteers, your generosity creates lasting impact. Choose the cause that speaks to you and become part of Kenya's journey towards a future free from rabies, preventable suffering, and animal cruelty. "Small acts of compassion create extraordinary change."

By choosing Tails of Care, you are partnering with an organization dedicated to measurable impact, transparent stewardship, and the long-term protection of both animals and humans alike.

"The greatness of a nation and its moral progress can be judged by the way its animals are treated."
