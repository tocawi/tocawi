Our Programs & Services

The ToCAWI Initiative tackles the complex challenges of rabies and stray animal populations through a holistic, impact-driven strategy. Our mission is built on three essential pillars: systematic Rabies Control, compassionate Population Management, and transformative Education & Awareness. By unifying veterinary expertise with community action, we protect public health and ensure a safe, sustainable future for Kenya's animals and people alike.

Rabies Control

Vaccination Campaigns

We lead systematic mass vaccination campaigns across Nairobi and rural counties, eradicating rabies at the source through community clinics and door-to-door outreach to protect both dogs and people.

Basic Surveillance

Our data-driven model utilizes mobile tracking and community reporting to monitor rabies cases, ensuring rapid response and transparent impact reporting for our partners and local health authorities.

  • Over 15,000 vaccinations administered annually
  • Digital data tracking for 100% campaign transparency
  • Establishing safe corridors through high-density districts
  • Real-time reporting of suspected cases

Humane Population Control

We believe that managing dog and cat populations requires empathy combined with science. Our humane spay and neuter programs are designed to improve the collective health of strays while ensuring the long-term safety of Kenyan communities. By focusing on sustainable, compassionate stray management, we reduce local environmental burdens and champion a culture of care for all animals.

Community Education

Knowledge is the most effective tool for preventing rabies. Through ToCAWI's outreach and school programs, we educate thousands of children and families on animal behavior, safety protocols, and the absolute necessity of vaccination. By fostering empathy and understanding, we empower communities to protect themselves and their animals effectively.

How We Work

Vibrant Partnerships

We build sustainable solutions by collaborating deeply with local veterinarians, government agencies, and elders to ensure every initiative is community-led and culturally respected.

Volunteer Mobilization

Our passionate network of volunteers powers our door-to-door missions, organizing vaccination clinics and leading workshops that empower families with life-saving safety skills.

Data-Driven Planning

Using precise mapping and analytics, we target high-risk zones effectively. This data-first model ensures transparency and measurable impact in every community we serve.
